# Irish Rugby Football Union (IRFU)

The **Irish Rugby Football Union (IRFU)** (*Cumann Rugbaí na hÉireann*) is the governing body for rugby union across the entire island of Ireland (encompassing both the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland). Headquartered at Lansdowne Road in Dublin, the IRFU administers national teams, provincial structures, domestic club leagues, and grassroots rugby.

## History

### Formation & Early Years
Before the unification into a single governing body, rugby in Ireland was managed by two regional unions:
* **Irish Football Union:** Founded in December 1874, covering clubs in Leinster, Munster, and parts of Ulster.
* **Northern Football Union of Ireland:** Founded in January 1875, governing the Belfast area.

In **1879**, these two organisations merged to establish the **Irish Rugby Football Union (IRFU)**. Provincial branches were initially created for Leinster, Munster, and Ulster, followed by the Connacht Branch in 1885. 

In 1886, the IRFU co-founded the International Rugby Football Board (now **World Rugby**) alongside Scotland and Wales.

### All-Ireland Structure Post-Partition
Following the political partition of Ireland into the Irish Free State (now the Republic of Ireland) and Northern Ireland, the IRFU decided to maintain an all-island administrative structure across all 32 traditional counties. As a result, Irish national rugby teams draw players from two separate political jurisdictions.

To solidify ties between North and South, the IRFU acquired Ravenhill Stadium in Belfast in 1923, which hosted international fixtures alongside Lansdowne Road.

## Emblems, Flags, and Anthems

* **The Shamrock:** The shamrock has been a constant emblem of Irish rugby since 1874. The logo evolved from a five-sprig emblem to a four-sprig shield in 1898, and later into the modern three-shamrock crest.
* **The IRFU Flag:** Designed in 1925, the flag features a green field containing the coats of arms of Ireland's four traditional provinces (Leinster, Ulster, Munster, and Connacht) centered around the IRFU logo.
* **Anthems:** At home matches in the Republic of Ireland, both *"Amhrán na bhFiann"* (the national anthem) and *"Ireland's Call"* are performed. At away matches or fixtures played outside the Republic, *"Ireland's Call"* is sung exclusively as the unifying anthem for the whole island team.

## Structure & Provincial Rugby

The IRFU oversees four main provincial branches:

1. **Leinster Rugby** (12 counties)
2. **Ulster Rugby** (9 counties: 6 in Northern Ireland, 3 in Republic of Ireland)
3. **Munster Rugby** (6 counties)
4. **Connacht Rugby** (5 counties)

Since the advent of professional rugby union, the four senior provincial teams operate as professional entities competing in the **United Rugby Championship (URC)** and European Professional Club Rugby (EPCR) competitions.

### Domestic & Grassroots Participation
* **Affiliated Clubs:** Over 200 senior and junior clubs active across the four provinces.
* **All-Ireland League (AIL):** Formed in 1990 to provide a top-tier national club competition.
* **IRFU Exiles Branch:** A specialized branch based in Great Britain focused on identifying and developing eligible Irish-diaspora talent.

## National Teams

The IRFU administers national sides across men's, women's, and junior levels:

* **Men's National Teams:**
  * Ireland National Rugby Union Team (Senior Men's 15s)
  * Ireland Wolfhounds (A-Team / Select Team)
  * Emerging Ireland
  * Ireland National Sevens Team
  * Ireland Under-20s & Schoolboys
* **Women's National Teams:**
  * Ireland Women's National Rugby Union Team
  * Ireland Women's National Sevens Team
  * Combined Provincial Teams (Wolfhounds & Clovers in Celtic Challenge)
